There are several physical symptoms that are distressing and seriously affect daily life. The person feels extreme fear and concern for their health. He gets into disproportionate and constant physical thoughts. He spends a lot of time and research on his health. It is a state of thinking that he has or will have a serious illness. He / she commits inappropriate avoidance behaviour.
