Physiological, hormonal, psychological, and social changes occur in many areas of a person's life during pregnancy and after birth. With the disposition of the person, this process becomes smoother or more problematic. Symptoms such as sadness, insensitivity, fatigue, loss of energy, and sleep appetite disorders can be observed.
