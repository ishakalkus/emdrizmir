**WE CAN OVERCOME THE FEAR OF SEPARATION**  
If the fear of losing and separating from any of our mother, father, sibling, or other people we care about affects our routine life when we consider our daily life and school performance, it means that we experience "separation anxiety".

If we can find the reasons why we are experiencing this high level of anxiety, we can reduce the level of anxiety to a normal level.  
This can easily be achieved with the help of an expert.
