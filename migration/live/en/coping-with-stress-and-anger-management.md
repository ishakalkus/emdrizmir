If anger and stress are out of proportion to the community and your environment, you can get support. You may think you are unable to cope with stress and you may feel depressed. There may be symptoms such as a decrease in your interest in activities, showing avoidance behavior. In situations where you feel anger, you can get help for symptoms such as overreacting, turning to violence.
