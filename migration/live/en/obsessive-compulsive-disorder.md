They are repetitive, continuous thoughts (obsessions) or images that are forced and unintentional and cause significant anxiety and distress. Although the person tries to suppress the images, they cannot control them. He switches to actions (compulsions) to which he feels obliged to counter this obsession. Examples are hand washing, arranging, monitoring, uttering religious words, and counting. Thoughts and actions take up a lot of time and there is a decrease in functionality.
