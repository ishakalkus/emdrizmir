A counselling service should be sought when symptoms of communication problems, anger control, conflict, reconciliation, discussion, premarital counselling, unhappiness, and sexual problems are disproportionate. Help can be obtained in cases such as mother-father-child triangle problems, addiction problems, mother-father relationship problems and post-marriage divorce.
