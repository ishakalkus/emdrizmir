Congenital and environmental factors are types of disorders that can vary. There are different personality disorders under 3 headings.  
It is a wide-reaching area like paranoid, schizoid, schizotypal, antisocial, borderline, histrionic, narcissistic, avoidant, dependent, obsessive compulsive personality disorder. In general, a serious decrease in the functionality of the person and symptoms, which take a long time to complete, is observed.
