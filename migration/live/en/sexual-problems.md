Sexual disorders are divided into problems such as late ejaculation, difficulty or inability to maintain an erection, orgasmic disorders in women, low sexual desire disorders in men, premature ejaculation before one's will, and vaginismus. Vaginismus: It is also known as a pain penetration disorder in the reproductive organ. It is a condition of recurring pain when inserted into the vagina, pelvis, or organ. The muscles may be stretched or tightened significantly. Low sexual pleasure disorder in men and women; Symptoms such as apathy or low interest, low sexual desire, lack of reaction can be observed.
