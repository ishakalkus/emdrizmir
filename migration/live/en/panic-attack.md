**WE DON'T LEAVE OUR LIFE TO THE PANIC ATTACK**

Panic attack can manifest as an anxiety attack that develops suddenly and is intense.

If we suddenly have chest pain, sweating, frequent and strong breathing, and thoughts that we cannot survive at that moment, panic attacks can occur.

Before these attacks become common and take over our lives, the best option is to get help from an expert.
