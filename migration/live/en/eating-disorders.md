Eating disorders are divided into separate titles.

**Anorexia Nervosa:** Symptoms such as having a low body weight, restricting energy intake, fear of gaining weight, loading a disproportionate importance to shape and weight can be seen.

**Bulimiya Nervosa:** Repetitive binge eating periods, inability to stop eating, vomiting in order not to gain weight, excessive exercise may be seen.
