Uzm. Dr. Mehmet Oğuz

Psychiatrist

Mehmet Oğuz graduated from the Medical Faculty of Marmara University in 1999 and was promoted to doctor. Between 1999 and 2001 he worked as a general practitioner at the Azizler Village Health Center.

He then joined the Turkish Air Force in 2001 and worked in an ambulance aircraft and as a flight doctor.
In the meantime he successfully completed his psychiatry assistant at the Haydarpaşa Training and Research Hospital, which is subordinate to the Military Medical Academy Gülhane, and became a psychiatry specialist. During his service in military facilities, he has followed up many cases of post-traumatic stress disorder.

He left the Turkish Air Force in 2014 when he was in the rank of major and continued his career as a psychiatrist and psychotherapist in the private sector with his team of experts. EMDR therapy is used to treat many psychological problems. In addition, he applies psychodynamically oriented psychotherapies in therapy methods such as cognitive behavioral therapy and schema therapy.

He is a member of the Turkish Psychiatry Association and the EMDR Association and the EMDR Association.

Bachelor: Medical Faculty, Marmara University

Uzm. Dr. Nihan OĞUZ

Uzm. Dr. Nihan OĞUZ

I was born in Lüleburgaz in 1975.

I finished my primary and secondary education in Babaeski. In 1992 I founded the Marmara Medical Faculty (University).

In 2000, I started my Mental Health and Disease Education at Bakırköy Mental Health and Disease Education and Research Hospital. My thesis is the comorbidity of attention deficit in adults and hyperactivity disorder in bipolar mood disorder in remission. In 2005 I left Bakırköy Mental and Neurological Hospital.

From 2006 to 2009 I worked as a psychiatrist in the Erenköy Training and Research Hospital for mental and neurological diseases.

Between 2009 and 2013 I worked as a psychiatrist in the regional training and research hospital in Erzurum.

Between 2013 and 2016 I worked as a psychiatrist in the Ataturk Training and Research Hospital of the University of İzmir Katip Çelebi in the AMATEM- Alcohol and substance addiction treatmant center

I worked as a psychiatrist in Urla State Hospital between 2016 and 2020.
