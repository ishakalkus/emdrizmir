It can be seen in people who have experienced real or intimidating death, serious injury, sexual assault, or direct trauma. There are times when you may see one-on-one the event that happened to someone else, witness, or learn that traumatic events have occurred in their family and relatives. The traumatic event can turn into troubled memories and dreams. There may be dissolution reactions, as if the situation in question occurs again. Intense stress can be observed when people and situations meet that are interrelated.
