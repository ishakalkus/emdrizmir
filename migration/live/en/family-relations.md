**WE CAN SOLVE COMMUNICATION PROBLEMS IN THE FAMILY**

The problems we have with people we share the same home with can be very different.

We can have problems with our children due to generation conflicts.

We can have problems with our spouse for a number of reasons.

All of this is often intertwined and difficult for all family members. It causes a feeling of negligence and burnout.

In such cases, it is best to consult a professional advisor before making a sudden decision.

The counselor will act as a third eye to help family members see the factors preventing their communication and help individuals change their perspective and talk about problems with one another in healthy ways.
