**WE CAN LEARN ANGER CONTROL**

Some of us can get very angry about family, work, friendship, traffic, and community.

Anger can affect all of our relationships, happiness, work, or school performance.

Anger can be a learned behavior that can be traced back to the environment in which we have lived since childhood and the experiences we have gained.

In this case, we can change our minds and see events from a new perspective.

When our thoughts change, our feelings change, our behavior changes. We can learn to control anger.

We can apply to a specialist for a calmer, happier and more successful future.
